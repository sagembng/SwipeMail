SwipeMail

Glass, kept quiet.

Frosted surfaces for the controls, near-solid cards for the mail. The brand stays in the background so the emails stay in front.

Principles

Glass on chrome onlyTabs, dock and buttons are frosted. Email cards use a high-opacity glass so text never fights the background.
One colour per meaningBlue acts, green means read, yellow means starred, red means urgent. Nothing else is coloured.
Depth from the stackCards peek above the top one, like the inspiration, so the pile reads as "what is left".
